Overview

The goal of this project is to design and develop an innovative agentic learning experience that combines AI technology with Adobe's educational platform to create more personalized and effective learning journeys for users.

Project Brief

Adobe Learn is Adobe's comprehensive learning platform that helps users master creative tools and techniques. This project aims to enhance the platform with an AI-powered experience that provide personalized learning resources and intelligent content recommendations tailored to users' goals.

My Role

During my internship, I lead the end-to-end design process, from initial user research and concept development through to final prototyping and user testing. This is where my internship ended, and I passed off implementation to the development team.


Initial Website State

The problem

Creative learners—beginners, hobbyists, and aspiring professionals—are eager to grow their skills and/or build careers, but they face a fragmented and overwhelming learning landscape. With content scattered across tutorials, forums, and platforms, and with learning needs varying widely, it’s hard to find reliable starting points or clear paths that align with personal goals.

The opportunity

Offering high-quality, personalized, creator-led learning experiences that stand apart from generic AI-generated content, helping users feel confident in their subscription and supported throughout their creative journey.

Key Design Elements

Conversational Interface

Why this approach: Users are already familiar with conversational interfaces from tools like ChatGPT. By leveraging this familiar pattern, we reduce cognitive load and make the learning experience feel natural and approachable.

Key benefit: The flexible conversational format allows users to express their goals in their own words, while the AI can pull from Adobe Learn's extensive content library to build customized learning paths tailored to their experience level and objectives.

Personalized Learning Paths

Why this approach: Instead of forcing users to navigate through predefined categories, the conversational interface allows them to describe their goals naturally—whether that's "I want to create a portfolio website" or "learn photo editing for my business."

Key benefit: The AI dynamically constructs learning paths by curating relevant content from Adobe Learn, ensuring each path is unique to the user's stated goals, skill level, and available time commitment.

Always-Available Assistant

Why this approach: Learning doesn't happen in isolation—users have questions while actively working in Adobe products. A floating action button (FAB) provides immediate access to the assistant without disrupting their workflow.

Key benefit: Users can ask context-specific questions at any moment, whether they're stuck on a technique or want to explore a related skill. The FAB maintains visibility while staying out of the way until needed.

Contextual Learning Within Adobe Products

Why this approach: Learning happens best when it's contextual to the user's current task. Rather than forcing users to switch between separate apps, the interface integrates directly within Adobe's creative tools.

Key benefit: Users can learn and apply new techniques immediately without leaving their creative flow, reducing friction and increasing retention by connecting learning to real-world application.

Multimodal Input Support

Why this approach: Creative learners often think visually—they might want to show what they're working on or ask questions about specific visual elements. Supporting image uploads enables richer, more intuitive interactions.

Key benefit: Users can upload screenshots or project files to get tailored feedback and recommendations, making the learning experience more contextual and personalized to their actual work.

Iteration & Refinement

Dual Learning Options

After initial user testing, I discovered that not all users want or have time for a complete guided learning path. Many prefer shorter, bite-sized content they can consume on-demand. I designed two parallel experiences to accommodate different learning preferences.

Learning Path Option

Structured, guided journeys for users who want comprehensive learning experiences with clear progression and goals.
